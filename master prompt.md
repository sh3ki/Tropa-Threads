## Premium One-Page Thrift & Streetwear Landing Page
### React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS

You are a senior frontend engineer, creative director, UI/UX designer, brand identity designer, and ecommerce landing page specialist.

Your task is to **design and build a complete, production-quality, visually impressive SINGLE-PAGE landing website** for a fictional local thrift clothing business based in Laguna, Philippines.

Do not just create a basic website. Build a polished, editorial, responsive, interactive streetwear storefront with a unique brand identity, excellent visual hierarchy, beautiful typography, compelling photography, and refined micro-interactions.
